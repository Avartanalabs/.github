## 🏛️ Featured Platforms & Client Showcases

### 1. [Samarohh](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/samarohh) · [samarohh.com](https://www.samarohh.com)
> **Celebration Coordination & Guest Management Platform**
* **The Problem:** Multi-day celebrations (weddings, galas, retreats) struggle with spreadsheet confusion and chat group noise. Native apps face low guest adoption due to store downloads and account setups.
* **The Solution:** A mobile web companion where guests view itineraries, submit RSVPs, and register dietary preferences in seconds without downloading an app or setting a password.
* **Live Platform:** [www.samarohh.com](https://www.samarohh.com)
* **Showcase Repository:** [`Avartanalabs/samarohh`](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/samarohh)

### 2. [Anvay ERP](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/anvay-erp) · [anvay.avartanalabs.com](https://anvay.avartanalabs.com)
> **Cloud ERP for Commercial Operations & Supply Chains**
* **The Problem:** Retailers, wholesalers, and distributors often navigate disjointed operations between single-PC desktop billing software and costly enterprise suites.
* **The Solution:** A unified cloud ERP bringing together fast point-of-sale billing, multi-warehouse stock replenishment, tax compliance, and automated invoice delivery.
* **Live Platform:** [anvay.avartanalabs.com](https://anvay.avartanalabs.com)
* **Showcase Repository:** [`Avartanalabs/anvay-erp`](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/anvay-erp)

### 3. [Udai Kothi Heritage](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/udai-kothi) · [Live Showcase](https://avartanalabs.github.io/udai-kothi/)
> **Luxury Hospitality Digital Flagship**
* **The Problem:** Heritage boutique hotels surrender substantial revenue to high-commission travel aggregators when limited by rigid, generic booking engines.
* **The Solution:** A custom editorial web experience featuring fast page delivery, responsive interactions, and a direct VIP Concierge reservation drawer.
* **Live Showcase:** [avartanalabs.github.io/udai-kothi](https://avartanalabs.github.io/udai-kothi/)
* **Showcase Repository:** [`Avartanalabs/udai-kothi`](https://github.com/Avartanalabs/udai-kothi)
